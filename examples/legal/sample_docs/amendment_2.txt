AMENDMENT 2 TO SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Amendment 2 is made on October 3, 2026 between Orion Robotics, Inc. ("Orion") and Vega Components Corp. ("Vega"), and amends the Supply Agreement between Orion and Vega dated March 1, 2025.

1. Section 6 of the Supply Agreement is amended so that, from January 1, 2027, Vega's total liability to Orion under the Supply Agreement will not exceed $2,000,000.

2. All other terms of the Supply Agreement remain in effect.
