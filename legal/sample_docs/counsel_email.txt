Subject: Orion diligence: Vega supply agreement

Sam,

We finished our review of Orion's Supply Agreement with Vega. Two points matter for the Acme deal.

First, the agreement lets Vega terminate if a competitor of Vega acquires Orion. Acme does not compete with Vega, so we do not think Vega can use that clause against this deal.

Second, Orion needs Vega's written consent to assign the agreement, and the deal is structured as an asset purchase, which is an assignment. Our view is that Vega's consent is a closing risk for the Acme deal, because the supply agreement covers most of Orion's motors.

We recommend that Orion send Vega the consent request by November 20, 2026, so Vega's 15-business-day answer period ends before the planned closing.
