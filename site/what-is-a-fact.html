What is a fact?

In Factoid, a fact is one short, self-contained sentence with no filler, no unresolved pronouns, and no typed fields. Each fact keeps source quotes (with who said them and when), links to the entities it is about, traits, and time properties. When a later passage updates or disagrees with an earlier one, Factoid resolves the pair on write, retiring superseded facts or flagging contradictions.
